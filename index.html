A chibi customer orders at the counter inside the shop. Their recipe appears for 10 seconds (a little longer in later stages) — ingredients and their order. Memorize it!

Click through to the potion room — the recipe stays at the counter. Drag the floating ingredients into the cauldron in the exact order you remember, before the customer's patience timer runs out!

Nail the recipe to earn Brew Points and serve the customer. Every few successes unlocks a brand-new potion for your recipe book.
